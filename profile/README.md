# FINN Partners

> More than an agency. Champions of change.

FINN Partners is one of the fastest-growing global, independent marketing and
communications agencies in the world, with a heart and a conscience. We serve
400+ world-class brands through bold creativity, informed strategy, hands-on
partnership and highly specialised expertise, guided by a values-driven culture
that champions integrity, collaboration, and innovation. As ONE FINN, our teams
across the Americas, EMEA and APAC are united by a shared vision and a relentless
commitment to driving positive change.

## What our developers build

This is the home of the FINN Partners engineering team. Behind our campaigns and
client work sits a modern web platform that we design, build and run in-house:

- **The FINN WordPress platform.** A shared theme and plugin ecosystem
  (`fp-theme` and our `fp-*` plugins) powering client and corporate sites,
  hosted and deployed on Pantheon.
- **Modern web applications.** Full-stack apps built with Next.js, TypeScript
  and Postgres for internal tooling and client products.
- **Engineering standards.** Typed, tested and reviewed code, with shared
  conventions across every repository so teams worldwide build the same way.
